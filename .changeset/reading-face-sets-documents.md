Say what the reading face is for

The reading typeface sets documents, not replies: a response in document
mode takes `--lucet-font-prose`, while a chat reply is interface and
stays in the UI sans beside the controls. That was always the behaviour;
the note now says so, so the narrow reach of the prose slot reads as the
decision it is rather than an under-application.
